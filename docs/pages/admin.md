# Admin pages (`/admin/*`)

`app/admin/layout.tsx` lets only `ADMIN` in (everyone else goes to `/dashboard`) and renders
`AdminChrome`: a sidebar with System Overview, Properties, User Management, Reports, Bank Transfers,
Security and Platform Settings, a slide-over drawer on a phone, and the notification bell.
Components are in [`../components/admin.md`](../components/admin.md).

| Route                     | File                              | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :------------------------ | :-------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `/admin`                  | `admin/page.tsx`                  | System Overview: platform totals from the server (`AdminDashboard`); each card opens a breakdown                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `/admin/breakdown/[type]` | `admin/breakdown/[type]/page.tsx` | The page a card opens: `users`, `properties`, `transactions` or `maintenance`, with server totals and a paged table                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| `/admin/properties`       | `admin/properties/page.tsx`       | The moderation queue: every listing as a list or grid, filtered by moderation status (Pending Review, Approved, Rejected, Flagged, Merged or All), a **Duplicate** badge, a review drawer (approve or reject a listing or one of its units, approve a staged edit, unpublish, re-approve, moderation notes) and a merge-duplicates dialog                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `/admin/properties/[id]`  | `admin/properties/[id]/page.tsx`  | One listing in the same management view the property team sees (`PropertyDetail`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `/admin/users`            | `admin/users/page.tsx`            | User management as cards or a table: search, role and status filters, a profile panel. Suspending, reactivating or changing a role asks for a reason and is written to the audit log                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| `/admin/reports`          | `admin/reports/page.tsx`          | Reports and analytics in five tabs: Overview, Financial, Users, Properties and Maintenance, with a month range                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| `/admin/payments`         | `admin/payments/page.tsx`         | Every bank transfer on the platform, for confirmation or rejection by someone other than the person who recorded it (`BankTransferReconciliation`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `/admin/security`         | `admin/security/page.tsx`         | The audit log: sensitive actions (suspensions, role changes, manual payments, invoice edits, reconciliations, payouts, retention and storage) with filters and expandable details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| `/admin/plans`            | `admin/plans/page.tsx`            | Plans & billing: the plan rules (enforce limits, whose plan units count against, what happens beyond the included units, what happens on a downgrade, grace days, what a company's people can see: their own properties or the whole company's), the plans (price per month and year, units, users, extra-unit price, monthly credits, bullet points, default, popular, switched off), the paid-services catalogue, the credit packs people can buy (with the switch that opens or closes the shop) and a form to add or take back credits by email. Everything is saved at once and logged                                                                                                                                           |
| `/admin/stamp-duty`       | `admin/stamp-duty/page.tsx`       | The stamping queue: agreements people paid Proplity to stamp (to do, done, cancelled). For each, the reference from the tax authority, the duty actually paid and the receipt file are recorded to mark it stamped; or it is cancelled with a reason and a refund is logged                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `/admin/agreements`       | `admin/agreements/page.tsx`       | Rent agreements, in three tabs: **Rules** (templates must be approved, the manager may type the owner's name, offer a caution deposit / agency fee / legal fee, when a rent starts, charge credits to make or upload an agreement, witnesses by email, stamp duty payment and fee, **renewing with a new agreement**, **reminders** and their days, **automatic renewal** and its window), **Jurisdictions** (the legal rules of each place: advance-rent cap and how strictly it is applied, notice periods, areas the law does not cover, deed threshold, stamp duty note, source, lawyer reviewed) and **Templates** (the clauses of each wording, locked / editable / optional, versions, approve, new version, archive)          |
| `/admin/settings`         | `admin/settings/page.tsx`         | Platform Settings, four switches (plus the three cards below): auto-complete a repair when the vendor invoices; ask tenants to confirm finished repairs; a tenant's payment recorded by the team needs a second person; a vendor's payment needs a second person; three more cards: charge auto-pay tenants automatically, and delete identity documents when kept long enough, with the two retention periods (both off by default; each asks once more before turning on, and says when the deployment flag holds it shut, ADR-0015), and the daily limit on new people a manager or landlord may invite by email (10 by default), with a switch for whether they may invite on a property kept for management only (on by default) |
