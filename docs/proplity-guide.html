What is Proplity?

Proplity is an all-in-one property management platform built for the Nigerian real estate market. Think of it like a digital property office that connects everyone involved in renting a home.

🏠
Tenant
Pays rent, reports repairs, manages lease
🏗
Landlord
Owns properties, tracks revenue
👔
Manager
Runs daily operations for landlords
🔧
Vendor
Fulfils repair and maintenance jobs
🛡
Admin
Oversees the entire platform

2.11Auto-Pay for Rent

A tenant can let Proplity pay each rent invoice automatically. Auto-pay reuses the card the tenant last paid with online, so one card payment has to happen first.

  • Open Setup Auto-Pay on the dashboard Payment Information card. With no saved card it explains that one rent invoice must be paid by card first.
  • With a saved card it shows the card (last four digits and bank) and asks you to confirm with Enable Auto-Pay.
  • Once on, the card shows Auto-pay is active with a Cancel Auto-Pay link. Cancel any time.
  • The background charger (off until the operator sets AUTOPAY_WORKER_ENABLED) charges each open invoice once its due date arrives, never twice for the same invoice and day.
  • After three failed charges in a row auto-pay pauses and the tenant is notified to update the card or pay the invoice by hand.

6.2Properties Moderation, Reports, Audit Logs & Settings

Every property listing is reviewed before going live. Platform-wide reports, security audit logs (every action logged), and system configuration settings.

Platform Settings has four switches: Auto-complete maintenance jobs on invoice, and Ask tenants to confirm finished repairs (on by default). Turning the second off stops new questions; repairs already waiting for an answer can still be answered. The other two, Second person confirms what a tenant is recorded as paying and Second person confirms what is paid to a vendor, are on by default: a cash or transfer payment the property team records does not count until someone other than the person who entered it confirms it. Turn one off for a property with one person on the team and their entry counts at once (still logged, with a receipt).

6.4Duplicate Listings: Merge and Undo

When a new listing has the same address as an existing one it is marked Possible duplicate. The admin can merge it into the listing to keep.
