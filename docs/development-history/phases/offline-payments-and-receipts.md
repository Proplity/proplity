# Offline payments and receipts — "Paid another way", a transfers list, a receipt for every payment

**Status:** Built on `feat/offline-payments-and-receipts` (stacked on `feat/repair-follow-through`). **Date:** 2026-10-19. **Issues:** ISS-103, plus ISS-111 to ISS-115 found on the way. **Migration:** `20261019190000_payment_receipt_number`.

## Why

The API already took bank-transfer submissions with a proof file, reconciled them, and recorded offline payments, but no screen used any of it (only the tenant record's "record payment", reference only, no file), and no payment had a receipt anywhere. People who pay rent or a vendor's bill by transfer or cash had nothing to show and nobody could check it.

## What was built

- **"Paid another way" form** (`PaidAnotherWayForm.tsx`). A bank transfer or cash payment recorded with amount, bank, name on the account, reference, date and a receipt or proof file (optional, uploaded to the `reconciliation` folder). It records a `BankTransferSubmission` and changes nothing else. The tenant's payment history has an **I paid by bank transfer** button and a **Transfers you recorded** list; the property team uses the same form on a repair's vendor invoice (`InvoicePayments.tsx`, under the invoice in Cost Tracking) to record paying the vendor, and the repair page lists transfers waiting for confirmation there. Rent paid in cash or by transfer is still recorded straight away with **Record payment** on the tenant record (the existing, audit-logged route `POST /invoices/[id]/payments`; no second person), see ISS-117.
- **Transfers list** (`BankTransferReconciliation.tsx`): `/dashboard/reconciliation` for managers and landlords, `/admin/payments` for admins (a **Bank Transfers** tab in both navs). Tabs: waiting, confirmed, rejected. **Confirm payment** creates the `Payment`, updates the invoice and tells everyone concerned; **Reject** needs a reason and tells the person who recorded it.
- **Nobody confirms their own entry.** `reconcileBankTransfer` refuses `APPROVE` when the actor recorded the submission (403 "Someone else must confirm it"). They can still withdraw it by rejecting it. When the submitter is the only person who could confirm (a manager on a property with no landlord), the admins are told in the app instead.
- **A receipt for every payment.** `Payment.receiptNumber` (`RCT-` plus ten characters), generated by the database exactly like `invoiceNumber` (rule 11), and back-filled for every earlier payment by the migration. `GET /payments/[id]/receipt` and the page `/receipts/[id]` (`ReceiptView.tsx`: who paid whom, how, the reference, who recorded and confirmed a transfer, what is left on the invoice, the proof file, print or save as PDF). It shows names only, never an email, phone number or provider data. Receipt links are in the tenant's payment history, the vendor's invoices, the repair page's invoices (`InvoicePayments.tsx`), the transfers list and the notifications.
- **Who may open a receipt** (`lib/receipts.ts`): admins, the lease's tenant, the invoice's own user, the job's vendor, and the property's manager and landlord. Anyone else gets 404 (not 403, so receipt ids cannot be probed). A tenant is not a party to a vendor's invoice and cannot open that receipt. The page is `/receipts/[id]`, outside `/dashboard` and `/admin` because every role opens receipts there; `proxy.ts` guards it and sends a signed-out visitor to sign in and back.
- **Notifications.** Recording tells the other people on the property team (with email, linking the transfers list). Confirming tells the person who recorded it, the lease tenant and the vendor, each linking the receipt. A card payment through the webhook and a payment recorded by hand on a repair invoice tell the vendor.
- **Attention rows.** Team: Transfers to confirm. Admin: Transfers waiting for you.
- **Files.** The gateway's rule for the `reconciliation` folder now also lets the lease tenant and the vendor read the proof once the submission is approved (besides whoever uploaded it and the property team).

## Decisions worth remembering

- **Confirmation is a second person, not a role.** The rule applies to everyone, admins included: whoever recorded an entry cannot confirm it. A manager's entry on a property with no landlord is therefore confirmed by an admin (the journey's property is set up that way).
- **Receipts are per payment, not per invoice.** A part payment gets its own receipt showing what is still owing.
- **Receipt access is 404 for non-parties**, the same as other private records.
- **Payments are returned through one select** (`lib/api/paymentSelect.ts`, `PUBLIC_PAYMENT_SELECT`). A new route that returns payments must use it; `rawProviderPayload` and the virtual account number never leave the server.
- **No migration for the proof file.** The proposal expected one; `BankTransferSubmission.proofFileUrl` already existed. The only migration is the receipt number.

## Known limits

- A transfer can only pay one invoice; paying several with one transfer means recording each.
- There is no receipt for a payment that was never recorded (cash handed over and never entered).
- The proof file is whatever the payer uploaded; nothing checks it matches the amount or the bank statement. A person reads it before confirming.
- A confirmed entry cannot be undone from the screen; a correction is a new, opposite entry recorded by the team.
