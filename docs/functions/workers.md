# Background workers (`lib/workers/`)

Ten daily jobs. Each is a function returning a small result object; none is meant to be called from a
screen.

## How they run

- `vercel.json` schedules one call a day (`0 2 * * *`) to `GET /api/v1/cron/all`. Vercel's Hobby plan
  allows only two once-a-day crons, so that one route fans out to every job **in dependency order**.
- `app/api/v1/cron/[job]/route.ts` also runs a single job by name. It accepts `GET` with
  `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>` (what Vercel Cron sends) or `POST` with `x-cron-secret`.
- One failing job does not stop the others; failures are collected and the response is a 500 if any
  failed, so the platform's cron log shows it.
- `lib/workers/auth.ts` (`verifyCronSecret`) compares the secret in constant time and throws at load
  in production when `CRON_SECRET` is unset.
- CLI wrappers for six of them are in `scripts/workers/` for manual runs (`pnpm exec tsx scripts/workers/<name>.ts`).

| #   | Job                               | File                               | Exports                                                                                                                                                                      | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | `rent-invoicer`                   | `rentInvoicer.ts`                  | `runRentInvoicer`                                                                                                                                                            | Raises the next `RENT` invoice (and the recurring `SERVICE_CHARGE` line) for each active rent whose cycle is due, and tells the tenant. Idempotent: it checks for an invoice at the computed due date first. It advances **one billing cycle per run**, so a rent several cycles behind catches up over several runs                                      |
| 2   | `autopay-charger`                 | `autopayCharger.ts`                | `runAutopayCharger` Acts only when `AUTOPAY_WORKER_ENABLED=true` and the Platform Settings switch is on (ADR-0015); otherwise it reports.                                    | Charges due invoices on active auto-pay mandates (Paystack charge authorization, or the mock gateway), pauses a mandate after repeated declines and audit-logs each attempt. **Off** unless `AUTOPAY_WORKER_ENABLED=true`; it has never run against live Paystack                                                                                         |
| 3   | `overdue-flagger`                 | `overdueFlagger.ts`                | `runOverdueFlagger`                                                                                                                                                          | Marks unpaid invoices overdue once the rent's grace period has passed, sends one payment reminder (a notice, an in-app notification and an email to the tenant and the property team) and adds a **one-time** late fee for `RENT` invoices by the rent's rule (a percentage or a flat amount, never both, never on a late fee itself)                     |
| 4   | `reminders`                       | `reminders.ts`                     | `runReminders`                                                                                                                                                               | A reminder for unpaid rent and charges due in the next 3 days, and for rents ending within 60 days. One per invoice and one per rent, however often the job runs (it skips a recipient who already has a notification with that title)                                                                                                                    |
| 5   | `maintenance-schedule-dispatcher` | `maintenanceScheduleDispatcher.ts` | `runMaintenanceScheduleDispatcher`                                                                                                                                           | Raises a repair for every active recurring schedule that is due and moves the next due date on (`lib/maintenanceSchedule.ts`). On an occupied unit the repair is attributed to the tenant, who is told; on a vacant or unrented unit it becomes a **work order with no tenant**                                                                           |
| 6   | `access-code-expiry-janitor`      | `accessCodeExpiryJanitor.ts`       | `runAccessCodeExpiryJanitor`                                                                                                                                                 | Marks active gate codes past their end time `EXPIRED` (codes are never deleted, so the access log survives)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 7   | `payment-reliability-scorer`      | `paymentReliabilityScorer.ts`      | `runPaymentReliabilityScorer`                                                                                                                                                | Rates each active or expired rent's `paymentReliability` (Excellent, Good, Fair, Poor) and `riskScore` (Low, Medium, High) from the share of its past-due `RENT` invoices paid on time, late or missed. A documented heuristic, **not** the AI late-payment prediction the PRD describes. Runs after the overdue flagger so it sees the resulting history |
| 8   | `property-trust-scorer`           | `propertyTrustScorer.ts`           | `runPropertyTrustScorer`, `computeTrustScore`, `TRUST_WEIGHTS`                                                                                                               | Recomputes `Property.trustScore` from signals the platform records: 40 base, up to 20 for verified owner and manager, 15 for repair follow-through, 15 for rent reliability and 10 for occupancy. The weights are the one place to tune it                                                                                                                |
| 9   | `storage-janitor`                 | `storageJanitor.ts`                | `runStorageJanitor`                                                                                                                                                          | Removes uploads that were signed but never completed, older than 24 hours (`cleanupStalePending`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 10  | `retention`                       | `retention.ts`                     | `runRetention` Acts only when `RETENTION_ENABLED=true` and the Platform Settings switch is on; the two periods come from Platform Settings (ADR-0015); otherwise it reports. | Deletes identity documents (the `profile` and `applications` folders) once there is no reason to hold them: the tenancy ended over 12 months ago, or every application was rejected and the latest decision is over 90 days old (ADR-0012). It only deletes when `RETENTION_ENABLED=true`; otherwise it reports what it would remove                      |
